💰 Your cost — capped & transparent
Pay per click (CPC). Impressions are free; clicks are what you pay for.
You set the cap. Total spend never exceeds your budget — start with the system-recommended amount, adjustable anytime.
Weekly or one-off. Weekly budget auto-resets every Monday; one-off campaigns stop automatically at expiry or when budget is used up.
Automatic bidding. The system adjusts CPC by competition and your budget to maximise conversions.
Simple billing. Daily cost is auto-deducted from earnings at noon next day (T+1); ad vouchers offset part of the cost.
